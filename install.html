Get MapAVet on your phone

Never far from an open door. · free · about 30 seconds

Not your regular mobile app (good to know)

MapAVet is a web app. Instead of downloading it from a store, you save it to your home screen from Safari or Chrome.

It takes a fraction of the space of a normal app, updates itself, and once it is on your home screen, clinics near me, your saved maps and tap-to-call all work with no signal at all.

You're all set - MapAVet is installed. Open it from your home screen. The first time, tap Find clinics near me and allow location.
