Privacy

← back

VetAlong does not collect, store or transmit personal information, and has no accounts.

Location. When you tap "Nearest overnight doors to me now" or "Near me", the app asks the phone for its position and uses it on the phone to sort clinics by distance. The position is not sent anywhere and is not kept.

Saved maps. Maps you add are stored in this app's own storage on your phone. They are not uploaded. Removing the app removes them.

Third-party services. Map background tiles are requested from Esri and the U.S. Geological Survey when you look at a map with internet access; those requests carry the map area being viewed. The live builder (vetlocator.streamlit.app) is a separate web page: addresses you type there are sent to routing and geocoding services (OSRM, U.S. Census Bureau, ArcGIS, Photon, OpenStreetMap) and, when configured, Google Places, to produce the map. It stores nothing about you beyond temporary caching needed to build the map.

Clinic information comes from public listings and is provided as a convenience; always call ahead. Nothing here is veterinary advice.
